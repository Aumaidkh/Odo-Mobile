FAQ

Questions worth
asking first

Where do the city prices come from?

From bills other Odo owners have scanned in your city. Every benchmark is shown with how many bills it is based on and a confidence level — when there is not enough data, Odo says so instead of guessing.

Does Odo sit inside my payment?

No. You pay however you already do — cash, UPI, card. Odo never handles the transaction. If you turn on auto-detect, it reads the payment notification after the fact, nothing more.

What happens to my bills and documents?

They stay yours. Odo reads them to pull out amounts and dates, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

Does it work if I pay in cash?

Yes. Photograph the pump display or the bill and Odo reads the numbers off it. No digital trail needed.

Is the Health Score an industry standard?

No — it is our own measure of how well a car has been kept, built from service regularity, documents, cost fairness and odometer consistency. The breakdown shows exactly how it is calculated.
